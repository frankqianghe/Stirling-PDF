Summary: Plex PDF processes your PDF files entirely on your local device. We never upload or access your document content. We collect limited technical data (license info, anonymized usage stats, crash reports) solely to operate and improve the product.

Wenx Studio LTD. ("Plex PDF", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Plex PDF desktop application and the website at https://plexpdf.wenxstudio.ai (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

By using Plex PDF, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Purchase Information: When you buy a license, our payment processor collects your name, email address, billing address, and payment details. We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Contact Form Submissions: When you contact us, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Support Requests: Information you voluntarily provide when requesting technical support.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Operating system version, device model, and hardware specifications necessary for license validation and compatibility checks.
  • Application Usage Data: Feature usage statistics (e.g., which tools are used, frequency of use), anonymized and aggregated to improve our product. This does NOT include the content of your PDF documents.
  • Crash and Error Reports: Diagnostic information when the application encounters an error, including stack traces and system state. No document content is included.
  • License Validation: We periodically validate license keys to verify legitimate use. This involves sending an encrypted license token to our servers.
  • Website Analytics: Our website uses standard analytics tools (page views, referrer URLs, browser type) to understand visitor behavior and improve the site.

1.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • ✅ Document Content: Plex PDF processes your PDF files entirely on your local device. We do not upload, access, read, or store the content of your PDF documents.
  • ✅ Keystrokes or Input Monitoring: We do not log keystrokes or record screen activity.
  • ✅ Personal Files: We do not scan or access any files on your computer beyond the PDFs you explicitly open within the application.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party services for payment processing, analytics, and hosting. These providers are contractually bound to use your data only for the services we request.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies to remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and maintain session state. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some website features may not function properly without them.

6. Data Security

  • Encryption: Data transmitted between the application and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2+.
  • Local Processing: PDF documents are processed entirely on your device. No document content is ever transmitted to our servers.
  • Access Controls: Internal access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

California Residents (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

EU/EEA Residents (GDPR): Our legal basis for processing is your consent and our legitimate interest in providing and improving the Service.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@wenxstudio.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Plex PDF is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in Canada, the United States, or other countries where our servers or service providers are located. By using Plex PDF, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for cross-border data transfers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date, and displaying a notice within the Plex PDF application. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
