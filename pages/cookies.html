This Cookie Policy explains how Plex PDF ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website https://plexpdf.wenxstudio.ai.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to website owners.

Cookies We Use

CategoryPurposeExamplesDuration
Essential Core website functionality — session management, security, the download consent flow Session cookies Session (deleted when browser closes)
Analytics Understand visitor behavior and improve the website Google Analytics (_ga, _gid) Up to 2 years
Preferences Remember your settings and choices Language preferences Up to 1 year

Plex PDF Desktop Application

The Plex PDF desktop application does not use cookies. It does not embed any web tracking or advertising technologies. All PDF processing happens locally on your device.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome — Manage cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox — Clear cookies
  • Microsoft Edge — Delete cookies
  • Safari — Manage cookies

⚠️ Note: Disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website, including the download consent flow. If download fails after disabling cookies, please re-enable essential cookies or use a different browser.

Do Not Track

Our website does not respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser signals at this time. We honor DNT signals where required by applicable law.
